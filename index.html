Research Interests

Keywords: LLM Evaluation, LLM Agents, Multi-Agent Systems, Model Training, Model Compression

My primary research focus is evaluating and understanding AI systems, from large language models to autonomous agents and multi-agent systems. As these systems take on increasingly complex and open-ended tasks, I am interested in how we can rigorously measure what they actually do well, where they fail, and why. Beyond evaluation, I also work on building LLM-based agent systems and on model training and compression to make them more capable and efficient.

In particular, my research interests lie in:
  • Evaluation methodologies and benchmarks for LLMs and AI agents
  • LLM-based agents and multi-agent systems
  • Reliability, robustness, and biases of AI systems
  • Model training and compression
